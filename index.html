Problem

Students often take more food than they can consume, resulting in avoidable leftovers and food wastage.

Solution

PortionWise uses previous consumption and leftover patterns to recommend an appropriate portion before a meal and learns from post-meal feedback.

Goal

Prevent food waste at the consumption stage through personalized portion recommendations and responsible eating habits.

Why it matters

~1/3

of all food produced worldwide is lost or wasted (FAO estimate).

Water, land, energy

are used to grow food that is never eaten.

120 meals

could be saved per month in a hostel mess if leftovers fell by 10% (sample estimate).
