About food waste

What is food wastage?

Edible food that is thrown away instead of being eaten. In canteens, much of it is plate waste from oversized portions.

An environmental problem

Rotting food in landfills releases methane, a strong greenhouse gas.

Wasted resources

Every wasted meal also wastes the water, energy, land and labour used to produce, cook and serve it.

How portion control helps

Taking only what you can finish prevents waste at the last stage, when the food has already used every resource.
