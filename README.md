# PortionWise — Smart Portion Recommendation System

## 📌 Project Overview

**PortionWise** is a smart web-based platform designed to reduce food wastage in college canteens and hostels.

The system recommends an appropriate food portion to users based on their previous consumption and leftover patterns. After a meal, users can provide feedback about their portion and the amount of food left. This information can be used to improve future portion recommendations.

The project focuses on preventing food waste at the **consumption stage** while encouraging responsible food consumption.

---

## 🌱 Problem Statement

Food wastage is a major environmental and sustainability concern. In college canteens and hostels, students may take more food than they can consume, resulting in unnecessary leftovers.

This waste also represents the unnecessary use of:

* Water
* Energy
* Land
* Food resources
* Labour

There is a need for a simple technology-based solution that encourages users to take an appropriate portion and reduce avoidable leftovers.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

PortionWise provides personalized portion recommendations based on a user's:

* Hunger level
* Previous portion size
* Previous leftover amount
* Food consumption patterns

After the meal, users provide feedback on whether the portion was sufficient and how much food was left.

### System Flow

**Previous Consumption → Portion Recommendation → Meal → User Feedback → Improved Future Recommendation**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Smart Portion Recommendation

Users enter their meal and previous consumption information to receive a recommended portion.

### 2. Meal Feedback

Users can report whether their recommended portion was:

* Too little
* Just right
* Too much

They can also indicate how much food was left.

### 3. Personal Waste Dashboard

The dashboard displays sample statistics such as:

* Meals tracked
* Meals finished
* Average leftover
* Food saved
* Estimated environmental impact

### 4. Food Waste Calculator

Users can enter the amount of food taken and the amount left over to calculate their food-waste percentage.

### 5. Sustainability Information

The website explains the environmental impact of food waste and how responsible portion selection can help reduce it.

## 📊 Recommendation System

The current website is a **prototype**. JavaScript-based logic is used to simulate personalized recommendations using sample consumption and leftover data.

The recommendation considers factors such as:

* Hunger level
* Previous portion
* Amount of food previously left
* Consumption history

The prototype demonstrates how a future system could incorporate machine-learning-based personalization.
